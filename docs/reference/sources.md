# Sources and scope

The Pact uses external sources to inform decisions, but external guidance is not silently
converted into a universal requirement.

For every active source, record:

- canonical URL or publication reference;
- publisher and document title;
- version or revision date;
- date reviewed;
- section relied upon;
- applicability and known limitations;
- the local Pact control or policy informed by the source.

Protocol requirements apply only within the protocol, version, and role that define them.
Vendor recommendations retain their original scope. Local policy is labelled as local
policy. Historical references are not evidence of current behaviour until revalidated.
